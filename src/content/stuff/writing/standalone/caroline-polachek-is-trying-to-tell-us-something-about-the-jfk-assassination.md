Pop chanteuse Caroline Polachek is no stranger to misdirection. In 2014 she adopted the pseudonym "Ramona Lisa" and engaged in live performances among body-doubles. The choreography for these performances emphasized [the confusion of identity](https://youtu.be/whZZvS7I-VI?si=39qF6xvZXPVc0-3F); the stage makeup exploited human pareidolia to disorient the viewer.

Even within the ouvre of Chairlift (Polachek's band with Patrick Wimberly), songs such as "Amanaemonesia" and "Met Before" deal in occult themes, if couched in the science of psychology and quantum physics.

Performing under her given name implied a more personal and confessional direction for her solo career after the disbandment of Chairlift. And such an interpretation was bourn out through 2019's _Pang_, which contained many allusions to interpersonal relations and desire.

_Desire, I Want To Turn In To You_ confounds, beginning even with its very title: who or what is Desire? How can one 'turn in to' it? Indeed, much of Polachek's lyrics seem opaque at first blush. It is my contention, however, that _Desire_ only __reveals itself fully through the interpretive lense of the JFK Assassination__.

Below you will find annotated selections of _Desire_'s lyrics with annotation explicating their significance. I believe the reader will find the exegesis speaks for itself.

## __what i'm watching__
### _Cure_ (1997)
My favorite Kiyoshi Kurosawa movie screened at my local indie cinema-- on my birthday no less.

Kiyoshi Kurosawa is a frustratingly 50/50 director to me. _Cure_ is an incredible movie, but I absolutely hated _Pulse_ (2001). Then he won me back with _License to Live_ (1998), which is somehow a comedy with the exact style and tone of his thrillers. But then _Charisma_ (1999) sucked.

I don't think they know how to write, stage, film, color grade, soundtrack, foley and mix movies like _Cure_ anymore. It's just gorgeous and mesmerizing (lol).

## __what i'm playing__
### _Fire On The Mountain_
![](https://img.itch.zone/aW1nLzI4NTc0NjYzLnBuZw==/original/N%2FgsHW.png)

> JERRI: Sorry- this is just a new low for you-- is all.
> 
> RIPLEY: How so?
> 
> JERRI: Meeting a guy from a hookup app? Inviting said guy to the campsite?

> JERRI: Having your meet-cute during work hours, while a group of kids are visiting, _all_ during an active investigation?

> _That... does sound really bad when it's all laid out like that._

If _Fire_'s exposition feels slow, the rest of the chapter is a roller coaster. Much like the moment-to-moment life of Ripley, the script rubberbands from slapstick to somber, progressing through each misadventure of Campsite 7 at a clip.

_Fire_ is one to watch, not just because it's jumping a formidable shark(-- writing two snarky twinks in the same script; something possibly never attempted before in English-language canon--), but because it's dripping with style and intrigue. 

P.S: Ripley is not getting into heaven for being a Radiohead fan. But that's okay because then we'll be there together <3.

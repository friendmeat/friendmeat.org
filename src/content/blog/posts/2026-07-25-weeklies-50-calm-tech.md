## __what i'm reading__
### _The Dyke and the Dybbuk_ by Ellen Galford
![The Dyke & The Dybbuk by Ellen Galford](https://m.media-amazon.com/images/I/51EYSp4P5cL._AC_UF1000,1000_QL80_.jpg)

A Jewish butch lesbian inherits a generational curse to be possessed by our narrator, a _dybbuk_. 

In _TD&TD_, even the extracorporeal aren't free of for-profit enterprise: The parallel universe of demons, djinn and dybbuks is one of org charts, trade conferences and office politics. Even as the _dybbuk_ enthralls her possessee with outbursts of Aramaic and nightmares of _coitus interruptus_, she must placate the corporate structure of her parent company. Of course Hell is full of lawyers.

I've never seen _Helluva Boss_ but I imagine this novel is in its DNA somewhere.

## __something i liked__
### calm technologies
I asked my barista about their flip phone and they informed me it was a CAT S22 Flip. We commiserated about the joy of disconnecting but the frustration of not having a good camera ready-to-hand. Perhaps there's a product here.

Later that day an article about ['calm technologies'](https://abhi.now/blog/calm-technologies/) popped in my feed. The author writes about products who selling points are their low-power-draw (Daylight Computer), single-purpose niches (refurbished iPods) and noninvasive designs (TRMNL). I hate to admit I was charmed by the understated luxury vibe all the products used in their marketing, but it really did put me in a good mood. There's a way out of this.

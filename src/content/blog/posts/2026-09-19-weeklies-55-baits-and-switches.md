## __what i'm watching__
### bait & switch #2: _8mm_ (1999)

The text of _8mm_ is supposedly about how corruption breed corruption. But I think it's really about othering the freaks.

The soundtrack heavily features vaguely oriental vocalizations that are so on-brand for a 00's terror-sploitation flic I actually forgot this was pre-9/11. It's a seemingly odd choice since the entire film is about white Americans doing American things, but the ethnic signaling is meant to reassure viewers of the foreignness of, if not the physical setting, then the psychological territory. The depravity is further othered by setting the extreme porn markets in smoky stonework alleys (as if it were an open-air souk "somewhere in the Middle East") and warehouses of VHSs guarded by gun-toting Hispanics. 

We're meant to find a parallel between the Nicolas Cage at the other end of the rabbit hole of desensitizing violence and the antagonists portrayed by Peter Stromare's amoral art-house director and James Gandolfini's sleazy pornographer (with absolutely diabolical facial hair). When the snuff-film actor (Chris Bauer) tells Cage that there's no root trauma or meaning motivating the on-film killings, that he does it "because he likes it", we're meant to identify that unmoored pleasure with Cage's own newfound depths of depravity. But that depravity reads ultimately as reparative: Cage takes revenge in service of the status quo, for a justice that means nothing to the victims but reassures us of a moral universe where evil men are killed by good men.

It has the typical 90s attitude toward not just fetish porn, but pornography in general: that it's the realm of madness, poverty, coercion, and abuse-- inherently corrupting.

There's also the oversimplification of the snuff-film supply chain. The titular 8mm reel that motivates the movie is created at the express request of a wealthy commissioner a mere two degrees removed from its creation, which we're reassured is an aberration. There's no suggestion of the much more unsettling reality we've seen play out in the wake of Epstein, or from the [Franklin affair](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Franklin_child_prostitution_ring_allegations) (currently being covered in the _Raising Hell_ series by [Cursed Media](https://www.cursedmedia.net/)), or the pre-internet web of pedophiles linked by [John David Norman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_David_Norman): that the abuse and violence doesn't happen under extreme circumstances, but with approval both tacit and active of the most powerful people in the world.

Why am I so miserable all the time.

So essentially, the film promises us a romp through depravity and filth, but gives us nothing but yet another reification of the moral superiority of 2.5 white picket heterofences.

## __something i hated__
### Quadruple-factor Authentication
Through no fault of my company's own, I'm in a situation where I have to authenticate four times on two different devices to log in to Microsoft Intune.

Here's my process:
1. Open an Incognito window (So my normal account cookies don't mess up the log-in process for a separate, administrative account)
2. Sign in to Microsoft Intune
3. Get forwarded to my org's 2FA page
4. Use a TOTP because my phone hasn't been able to connect to the Wi-Fi at work for 2 years, so I can't approve push notifications.
5. Get forwarded to Microsoft's 2FA
6. Start a mobile hotspot on my desktop because I always forget that Microsoft uses mandatory push notifications instead of generic TOTP, so I have to be connected to the internet.
7. Key my PIN in to my phone to open Microsoft Authenticator
8. Key in the confirmation number generated by Microsoft
9. Key my PIN in again to authenticate the sign-in
10. I'm in.

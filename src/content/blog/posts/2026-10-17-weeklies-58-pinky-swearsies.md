The bulk of the book focuses on Helmreich's sociological investigation of Artificial Life (A.L.) research being conducted at the Santa Fe Institute (SFI). Helmreich finds that the SFI researchers speak of lofty aspirations but more often than not simply reproduce heterosexist presumptions about biology and the world in their supposedly 'tabula rasa' creations.
